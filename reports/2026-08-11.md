## Findings

### CVE-2026-23552 — Camel-Keycloak: Cross-Realm Token Acceptance Bypass in KeycloakSecurityPolicy

- **Is this a vulnerability?** `CONFIRMED`. `KeycloakSecurityPolicy` does not validate the JWT `iss` (issuer) claim against the Keycloak realm the policy is configured for. A token issued by realm A is silently accepted by a policy configured for realm B, breaking multi-tenant isolation — cross-tenant data access and privilege escalation where realms carry different role mappings. Tracked upstream as `CAMEL-22854`.
- **Does the affected range include our pinned version (4.8.8)?** `NOT-AFFECTED`. Published range is `>=4.15.0, <4.18.0`; our pin (4.8.8) is well below that window.
- **Is an affected component in `used_components`?** `NOT-USED`. `camel-keycloak` does not appear in `config/libraries.yaml`'s `used_components` list — moot given the version mismatch, but confirmed independently.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL — CVSS 3.1 9.1 (`AV:N/AC:L/...`, as published by the advisory; not independently re-derived).
- **Verdict:** **NOT RELEVANT** (neither affected nor used).
- **Sources:** [Apache Camel security advisory](https://camel.apache.org/security/CVE-2026-23552.html) · [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-23552) · [Jira CAMEL-22854](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/CAMEL-22854) *(camel.apache.org/issues.apache.org could not be fetched directly this run — see Source health; content corroborated via cached/aggregator search results only, cross-referenced across independent aggregators for consistency)*.
- **Official fix / backport assessment:** Not performed — neither `AFFECTS US` nor a high-severity `NEEDS REVIEW`, per methodology step 5's scope.
- **Why this is "new":** this identifier was not present under any form in `state/seen.json` prior to this run, so it is processed as new per the dedup rule, even though the advisory itself appears to predate this watch's earliest backfill (2026-07-15). Recorded now to close the gap.
