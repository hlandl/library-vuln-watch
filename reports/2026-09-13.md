## Findings

### CVE-2026-80351 — Apache Camel K: Maven configuration eval injection
- **Is this a vulnerability?** `CONFIRMED` — public CVE, CVSS v3.1 9.8 (Critical).
- **Affected range vs. our version:** N/A — this affects **Apache Camel K** (`apache/camel-k`), the standalone Kubernetes operator project, versions 2.0.0–2.9.3 and 2.10.1–2.10.2. Apache Camel K has its own release line (2.x) entirely separate from Apache Camel core (`apache/camel`, 4.x) that this watch tracks at pinned version **4.8.8**. Fixed in Camel K 2.9.3, 2.10.2, 2.11.0.
- **Component analysis:** Camel K is a Kubernetes-native operator that deploys and manages Camel integrations as a separate control-plane component; it is not one of the `camel-*` core artifacts in `config/libraries.yaml`'s `used_components`, and this deployment does not run the Camel K operator at all.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL (CVSS 9.8, per public reporting) — but irrelevant to us per the above.
- **Verdict: NOT RELEVANT** (different product entirely, not deployed/used).
- Sources: [OpenCVE](https://app.opencve.io/cve/CVE-2026-80351), [VulDB](https://vuldb.com/cve/CVE-2026-80351), [Security Online summary](https://securityonline.info/apache-camel-k-vulnerabilities/), [OffSeq Threat Radar](https://radar.offseq.com/threat/cve-2026-80351-cwe-95-improper-neutralization-of-directives-in-dynamically-evaluated-code-eval-f9e261d2783da649).
- No backport assessment performed (not relevant to our pinned artifact/component set; see step 5 of methodology — reserved for AFFECTS US / high-severity NEEDS REVIEW).

### CVE-2026-80352 — Apache Camel K: custom-resource YAML/code injection
- **Is this a vulnerability?** `CONFIRMED` — public CVE, CVSS v3.1 9.8 (Critical).
- **Affected range vs. our version:** N/A — same product (Apache Camel K, `apache/camel-k`), same affected range (2.0.0–2.9.3, 2.10.1–2.10.2), fixed in 2.9.3/2.10.2/2.11.0. Not Apache Camel core / our pinned 4.8.8.
- **Component analysis:** Vulnerability is in the operator's `Master` trait `serviceAccountName` handling — an authorized custom-resource author can inject arbitrary Kubernetes objects created with the operator's own privileges. Not a `used_component`; the Camel K operator is not deployed by us.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL (CVSS 9.8) — irrelevant to us per the above.
- **Verdict: NOT RELEVANT** (different product entirely, not deployed/used).
