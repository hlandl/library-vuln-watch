## Findings

### CVE-2026-80354 — Apache Camel K: secret-resolution authorization bypass
- **Is this a vulnerability?** `CONFIRMED` — public CVE, CVSS 8.1 (High), disclosed 2026-09-10.
- **Affected range vs. our version:** N/A — this affects **Apache Camel K** (`apache/camel-k`), the standalone Kubernetes operator project, versions 2.0.0–2.9.3 and 2.10.1–2.10.2. Apache Camel K has its own release line (2.x) entirely separate from Apache Camel core (`apache/camel`, 4.x) that this watch tracks at pinned version **4.8.8**. Fixed in Camel K 2.9.3, 2.10.2, 2.11.0 — same fix train as last run's `CVE-2026-80351`/`CVE-2026-80352` (also Camel K, published 2026-09-08).
- **Component analysis:** The vulnerability is in the operator's `Builder` trait `mavenProfiles` `ValueSources` handling — an authorization bypass (CWE-639) that lets a tenant reference secrets by name in the operator namespace, potentially reading secrets belonging to other tenants or operator components. Camel K is a Kubernetes-native operator/control-plane component, not one of the `camel-*` core artifacts in `config/libraries.yaml`'s `used_components`; this deployment does not run the Camel K operator at all.
- **Severity:** HIGH (CVSS 8.1, per public reporting) — irrelevant to us per the above.
- **Verdict: NOT RELEVANT** (different product entirely, not deployed/used).
- Sources: [Apache Camel security advisory](https://camel.apache.org/security/CVE-2026-80354.html), [VulDB](https://vuldb.com/cve/CVE-2026-80354), [Security Online summary](https://securityonline.info/apache-camel-k-vulnerabilities/), [OffSeq Threat Radar](https://radar.offseq.com/threat/cve-2026-80354-cwe-639-authorization-bypass-through-user-controlled-key-in-apache-software-foundation-48f7fa17cec9458f), [strix.ai](https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-80354).
- No backport assessment performed (not relevant to our pinned artifact/component set; see step 5 of methodology — reserved for AFFECTS US / high-severity NEEDS REVIEW).
